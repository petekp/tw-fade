Progressive mode needs a scrollport that allows direct spans, such as div. Direct spans are not valid children of native lists or tables.

What to look for

A good result loses detail by degrees.

  • The center stays crisp and still.
  • The last pixels land without a bright hard clip.
  • Selected edges have equal apparent strength.
  • Mixed and corner cases have no seam or halo.
  • Changing blur strength should preserve the fade size.
  • Buttons and scrolling still work through every sheet.
